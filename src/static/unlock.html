Keep a history of your searches?

The app can remember the searches you run, so you can open one again from the advanced search. It is off unless you turn it on, and you can change it or clear it at any time.

Stored only on this machine, inside your encrypted corpus. Never exported, never in a diagnostics bundle.

Where should your corpus live?

Everything this app collects — the encrypted corpus, its keys and its logs — lives in one folder. You can keep the default or put it on another drive.

A folder named “OOS data” is created inside it.

Create your corpus passphrase

Your corpus will be encrypted at rest by default (SQLCipher 4). Pick ONE stable passphrase — you will enter it at every start, like a user ID.

Choose something unique and remember it.

There is no recovery and no decryption alternative. A lost passphrase costs re-collection time — the corpus is rebuilt from the web, not from us.

Make it long — several unrelated words beat one clever word. If your database file is copied it can be guessed offline, so length is the real protection (not how fast this screen answers).
