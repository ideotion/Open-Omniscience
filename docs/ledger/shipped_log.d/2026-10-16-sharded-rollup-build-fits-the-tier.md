## 2026-10-16 — the keyword rollup build fits DuckDB's 1,004 MB tier (PR #1422)

`columnar.build_keyword_daily` stages mentions into `shards` tables by `keyword_id % shards`, aggregates them one at a time into `keyword_daily_building` and drops each stage once aggregated; the swap into `keyword_daily` stays one transaction, and a failed shard leaves the previous rollup and no stage or building tables. `plan_build_shards(rows, limit_mb)` derives the count from the limit (the smallest count whose predicted peak is under 70% of it, never past the count where another table costs more than it saves). A shard that raises `OutOfMemoryException` (matched by class name) is halved on `(keyword_id // shards) % parts` up to 16 parts, then `BuildDeclined("duckdb-limit")` carrying shards, shard and split into the kept stop; any other error is never retried. `BUILD_CODE_VERSION` is 2, so stops kept by the single pass are released; `status()["last_build"]` reports shards, splits, the predicted and observed peaks and the build time.

Measured on a seeded corpus of 12,000,000 mentions / 1,500,000 keywords / 10,386,404 rollup rows at a 1,004 MB limit (957.5 MiB), 2 and 4 threads: DuckDB peak 604 and 584 MiB, RSS 923 and 911 MB, build 87.1 and 84.6 s (2 and 4 threads) and 84.3 to 84.8 s in earlier runs, against 1,986 MiB / 2,467 MB / 83.6 s for the single pass, which stopped at a 2,000 MB limit (peak 1,898 MiB) and built at 2,400 MB. Content digest (10386404, 24000000, 12000000, 17997137587126, 95801022961174191706596220) and meta digest (1500000, 13832410933449212566372076) are identical between the single pass and the sharded build at 2 and 4 threads; against the live SQLite count, mentions are exact and distinct is the disclosed upper bound. At 500 and 420 MB the build declines cleanly as `duckdb-limit`. The user's 856 s benchmark cannot be reproduced here: the ratio (about 1.01 to 1.04) is measured, a build of about 865 to 890 s on the user's machine is inferred. 24 M mentions was measured on a replicated stage file, not a streamed run. Constants measured on DuckDB 1.5.6.
