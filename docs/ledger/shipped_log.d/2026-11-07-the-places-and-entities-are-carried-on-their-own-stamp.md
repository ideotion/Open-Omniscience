## 2026-11-07 — the places and entities are carried on their own stamp, and "owes nothing" is both stamps (R415; PR #1761)

**What was found.** One stamp table certified an article's keyword rows and its places and entities, with an engine id that does not cover the gazetteers or the www modules. R415 gave the places and entities their own stamp (`article_www_stamps`); the re-index PR that added it classified the table "not carried" and left the restore to follow, so until this change a restore carried places and entities on the keyword stamp alone and the backlog counted them as certified (named in the re-index PR and in R418; no release tag was cut between the two merges).

**What changed.** `src/backup/merge.py`: the carry takes each half on its own evidence. The plan walks both stamp tables by one cursor each and merges the two streams by article id, verifies the keyword half with the keyword digest and the places-and-entities half with the re-index PR's `www_inputs_digest` (called on the incoming row, never recomputed), and the keyword guards take only the keyword half; the windows write mentions, counters and the keyword stamp for the first and places, entities and the www stamp for the second, in the same window as the rows. "Certified" is both stamps in the backlog read, the stand-in count, the post-swap re-index's skip set and the batch status (a batch is complete only when every inserted article was carried completely). The merge deletes leftover stamps of both tables at the ids it inserts. New report keys: `www_engine`, `www_stamped`, `www_verified`, `www_planned`, `www_reason`, `refused.www_inputs_changed`, `refused.article_missing`, `carried.keyword_articles` and `carried.www_articles` (`carried.articles` now means carried completely).

**Pinned by** about 22 new tests in `tests/test_derived_carry.py` (the differential against a re-index now also compares the www stamps; a title edit keeps the places carried, a body or country edit refuses both halves; one half only; a different www engine; a backup before the stamp; a corpus without the table; an identity that cannot be computed; a mixed backup walked in chunks of two; orphan stamps; the rowid-reuse delete) and one in `tests/test_stand_in_rows.py`; 41 mutants, 40 killed, the survivor equivalent. **Stated limits.** See the lesson fragment of the same date.
