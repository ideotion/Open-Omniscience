## 2026-10-16 — one extraction at a time, process-wide: a lock around the whole `extract_article` call tree (PR #1424, provisional until the PR is opened)

The export lane's follow-up to the 2026-10-14 SIGABRT in `lxml/html/__init__.py:740` (the coordinator's conditions of 2026-10-16 20:32Z: measure what the lock costs and state it in the PR, register any trafilatura bound in the same commit, name the evidence as inferred, and add an OPEN_QUEUE item for subprocess isolation). `src/ingest/extract.py` gained `_LXML_LOCK`, a `threading.RLock` around the whole of `extract_article` (the body moved to `_extract_locked`; a blank page still returns before the lock is asked for). Every parse, tree walk and tree release of the app's HTML happens inside that call tree, so concurrent use of libxml2 is impossible from the app whatever the fault was; the result holds text only, so the trees are freed inside the lock.

`tests/test_extract_lock.py` (35 tests): both paths (the one-parse path and the two-call fallback) never overlap across 8 and 6 threads, the lock is released when an extraction raises (asked from ANOTHER thread, since an RLock would let the leaking thread back in), a nested call does not deadlock (a thread join with a timeout, so a regression fails instead of hanging), a blank page does not wait for it, and the trees are freed while it is held (a sentinel `__del__` asks `_is_owned()`), plus an AST walk of all of `src/` for every way into libxml2 and of `extract.py` for calls to trafilatura outside the locked functions, each proved on fixture sources. Seven mutants of `extract.py`, all killed.

Cost, measured on 4 CPUs (trafilatura 2.2.0, lxml 6.1.3, libxml2 2.14.6; `collect_throughput_bench.py threads`, a new mode, and `sweep`, unchanged): extraction alone, 24 pages of about 22,000 characters, best of 3, articles per second at 1 / 4 / 16 / 50 threads, unlocked 213.6 / 57.1 / 52.7 / 46.1, locked 231.6 / 218.1 / 221.0 / 209.4; 12 pages of about 120,000 characters at 1 / 8 / 50 threads, unlocked 55.8 / 12.2 / 10.9, locked 52.2 / 51.3 / 45.2. Whole pass (96 articles stored every run), 1 / 8 / 50 workers: fetch delay 0.02 s unlocked 3.85 / 5.35 / 5.19, locked 4.21 / 6.19 / 6.17; delay 1.5 s unlocked 0.51 / 2.40 / 3.77, locked 0.51 / 2.45 / 3.92. Again on trafilatura 2.3.1 (htmldate 1.11.0), 24 pages of about 22,000 characters at 1 / 4 / 16 / 50 threads: unlocked 262.5 / 96.8 / 101.1 / 98.4, locked 276.3 / 320.4 / 291.1 / 267.2. The lock is not a cost on this box; unlocked threads are slower (the reason is not profiled). A gc probe (48 extractions, `DEBUG_SAVEALL`) left zero unreachable objects.
