## 2026-10-16 — rollup decline kept across restarts, live budgets on Home and the map (PR #1422)

- **2026-10-16 · analytics/rollup (night PR, follow-on to the stoplist batch).** From the two diagnostics bundles of one 7 GiB install (1,004 MB DuckDB limit): the boot's rollup build ran 921 s and stopped at the limit, the stop lived in memory, so the next boot paid the same scan; with no rollup, Home's 15 s poll ran the live trending scan 533 times in a session (75% of its wall clock) because the client back-off counted refusals in a 60 s window and a deadline refusal is itself 60 s; the map had no deadline, cache or budget; one card (`rising_now`) spent the whole card audit's budget. Six things worth keeping. (1) The stop is kept in `rollup_stopped.json` (codes and numbers only, version-tagged) and released by a larger limit, offload becoming allowed, a corpus 5% smaller than at the stop, or another app/build version; never by the epoch alone, because `reindex-all` bumps it every 300 articles. The 5% is a judgement, stated as one. (2) Single-flight shares a compute between concurrent calls and does nothing for the call after a failed one: a hard-deadline 503 or a partial payload is replayed for 30 s from a store of its own, marked `replayed` with its age, and never enters the normal cache. (3) `/trending-windows` takes a 20 s live budget, shortest window first; an unfinished window is left out and named, and an answer with no window is "not computed" on both panels. (4) The Home back-off counts a 503 or a partial answer only; a statusless error (a restart, a render exception) must not stretch it. (5) A deadline cannot be nested, so the audit orders by name (`_RUN_LAST`); under pressure its surfaced list then describes its own pass, and says so. (6) A reviewer's read found the first drafts wrong in places the tests had not reached (a one-mention drop released the hold, the partial caveat named a cause that can be false, a race around the first read of the kept record, the benchmark timed a different query than the app serves); each fix has a test that fails without it. English partial caveats; the 12 locale keys, a build that fits the tier, month occupancy and a prune from the middle are named items in OPEN_QUEUE.
