## 2026-10-27 — `statement_deadline`'s docstring now says what it does not bound, with the measured size of that gap warm on plain stores and cold on a 9.9 M-mention encrypted one; no block the walks reached is held open by many distinct short statements (coordinator items 1 and 2 of 15:27Z, ruling R321; comment-only, with the Library mentions count re-measure as the second record in the bundle)

**The question (a follow-on to #1543 and #1563).** The progress handler's 20,000-opcode counter belongs to one prepared statement, so a block of many DISTINCT short statements runs whole under any deadline. Is any block in `src/` such a block at a size that matters?

**What was done (read and measure only; the only edit is a comment).** Every `statement_deadline` was forced to a millionth of a second by a wrapper installed before the app imports (a block asking for 0, disabled by design, left alone), and the time each block stayed open was recorded, split into SQL time, the longest single call, Python, the statement count and the distinct text count. Plain SQLite 3.45.1, warm, the app's schema, synthetic: 60,000 articles / 169,695 mentions (163 MiB) and 300,000 / 848,841 (794 MiB), each with the 327 parameterless GET routes (50 entered a block) and the 57 bundle members (32 touch `db`), and on the 300,000-article store 73 parameterised routes (42 entered a block) and two direct drives (`build_stories`, `content_share_estimate`). SQLCipher 4.12.0 on SQLite 3.51.1, **cold** (file evicted with `posix_fadvise(DONTNEED)` plus a fresh process per walk), 300,000 articles / 9,900,000 mentions (3,221 MiB), through the app's own engine: the bundle, the 50 routes that had entered a block warm (48 entered one) and the 42 parameterised routes that had (41 entered one); no as-shipped run on that store.

**Result.** No block the walks reached is held open by the class. The distinct-text count per block is bounded by the code and the schema (identical between the two plain stores in 77 of 81 cases, `source-audit.json` going DOWN from 26 to 9; a search for SQL that inlines a data value found none in a deadline block; no block had more than 285 texts at 9.9 M mentions); the largest is `schema-drift.json`, **285 inspector queries** over 63 tables and 182 indexes, **0.25 s** warm plain and **0.449 s** cold encrypted. The 32 database-touching bundle members each returned within **0.87 s** warm plain and **0.92 s** cold encrypted (13 `skipped-deadline`, 19 `partial-deadline`), against up to **14.1 s** as shipped (`performance.json`). At the warm plain rate of about 90 microseconds a statement, reaching the 60 s deadline would take about 660,000 distinct statements in one block; at the cold encrypted schema-drift rate (about 1.6 ms a statement) about 38,000.

**What changed (`src/database/maintenance.py`, comment only).** The `statement_deadline` docstring gains two paragraphs: WHAT IT DOES NOT BOUND (statements that each end under 20,000 opcodes, a one-opcode statement such as a bare `count(*)` or the aggregated `dbstat`, Python between statements; the handler is armed on the session's connection only; a loop asks `deadline_expired`) and HOW MUCH (the figures above, each labelled by store and size, pointing to the lesson). No behaviour changed and no per-statement expiry hook was added (a measured gap of 0.25 s does not pay for a change to the file the whole app runs through; a proposal if a block ever shows more than a few thousand distinct statements).

**Named limits (the table is in `lessons.d/2026-10-27-blocks-of-short-statements-timed-at-a-forced-deadline.md`; none is a defect to fix here).** The simplemma first-use load, 6.6 s warm plain and 6.13 s cold encrypted, inside `GET /api/insights/associations` (Python, once per process, routed to the keyword lane); the keyword export block, deadline 0 by design, **about 38 s per block** open at 9.9 M mentions cold (38.6 s in `/keywords`, 37.9 s and 38.4 s in `/performance`, which runs it twice; 4.7 to 6.0 s per block at 0.85 M warm plain); the `minhash` stretch of `corpus-coordination` (0.9 to 1.3 s warm, 400 documents); `dbstat` in `storage.json` (0.5 to 0.85 s, plain only, one statement); the stories YAML parse (0.655 s open at 1e-6 with 0.007 s of SQL); the app-state KV store's own connection (another connection to the same file) in three bundle members (0.31 to 0.56 s cold against 0.007 to 0.026 s warm), the keyword export's read snapshot, and the bundle members that touch no `db` (`selftests.json` 8.49 s and `merge-diag.json` 7.28 s cold, inside their warm spread of 7.2 to 11.4 s and 5.6 to 8.3 s over six plain runs); the framing route and seven of the 38 `_deadlined` callers not driven; the DuckDB rollup builds, invisible to the recording.
