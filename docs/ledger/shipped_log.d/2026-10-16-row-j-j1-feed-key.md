## 2026-10-16 — Row J J1: a source is keyed on (domain, feed), the schema and its safe rebuild

Row J of `RELEASE_0.5_GATE.md` (S05-10, Q1102 = b, R145). **Before:** `sources.domain` was UNIQUE, one domain held one feed, and 475 catalogue entries (BBC's and DW's language services, the lean-tagged spectrum catalogue) could never be registered. **After (J1, no new rows yet):** the key is `(domain, feed_key)`; the rebuild of `sources` keeps every child row; every by-domain lookup says which source it means; the restore-merge matches on the pair. Measured first on the production seeder's capture: 475 shadowed become 216 new rows, 235 tag merges, 24 unregistered; 52 feeds are already carried by several domains' separate rows, which is why a key on the feed alone could not be installed.
