## 2026-10-27 — how much log the swap window finds: measured on real restores and repairs of two plaintext stores, the "NOT measured" sentence of `swap_window` replaced by ranges, and the stale writer list corrected (coordinator item 1 of 20:42Z; ruled R341, a stated limit; comment-only src and ledger fragments)

**The question.** `swap_window`'s docstring said the restore and the repair fold the log into the file before the window, so the window finds only what was written between the fold and the window, and that how many frames a real restore leaves there was NOT measured. Item 1 of 20:42Z: measure it on a named mid-size store, with and without a writer.

**Measured** (real committed `run_restore` and real `repair.run` on a plan made by the real start-up check; plaintext SQLite 3.45.1; this box, 4 vCPU and 15 GiB on a virtual disk; a 214.2 MiB store of 60,000 articles and a 1,055.4 MiB store of 300,000; the log read as frames from the wal-index header, not as the `-wal` file's size, which is a high-water mark). With nothing else writing: 0 frames at the window in every run. With an ORM writer outside the corpus lease (one row every 20 ms, or 200 rows a commit): 0 to 1,174 frames (4.6 MiB of frames) at entry in 21 restores, that writer's rate times a 0.25 to 0.59 s gap between the fold and the window, which did not grow with the store; folded by the first `dispose_engine()` in 0.001 to 0.03 s warm and 0.41 s once cold. The repair's window found none, or the repair declined (`saved-during-the-copy`, an ungated committer). The large store's other cost is the file replace: 0.012 s at 214 MiB, 0.39 s warm and 0.46 s cold at 1,055 MiB, with no log.

**Changed.** `src/database/library_generation.py`, docstrings only (the AST without docstrings is identical to main's): the `swap_window` paragraph now carries the figures with store, size, box, engine and temperature, and "NOT MEASURED: encrypted stores, a bigger store, a heavier writer"; the module docstring's list of writers no gate holds is corrected (the DDL steps of `init_db`, `source_identity` included; `spell_index`, the `fts` rebuilds and the settings saves are gated). **Corrected before it shipped.** The item 1 report gave the log at entry as 0 to 4.8 MiB; 4.8 was megabytes (4,836,912 bytes), the figure is 4.6 MiB, and the largest `-wal` file seen at entry was 6.5 MiB (a cold run whose 1,655-frame peak had been checkpointed down to 96). The report carries a correction note. The raw outputs (`swap-window-log-at-entry-data/`, outside the repository) kept two records per repair cell, not three.

**Not measured, stated:** encrypted stores, a bigger store, a heavier writer, which shipped writers commit during a restore without being a registered job. A writer outside the lease loses what it commits after the working copy was taken (known design, the R174 family; no new proposal).
