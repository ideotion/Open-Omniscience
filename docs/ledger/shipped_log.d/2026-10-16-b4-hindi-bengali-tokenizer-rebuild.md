## 2026-10-16 — Hindi and Bengali search tokenizer, paid in the migration window (PR #1419)

Row B4 of `RELEASE_0.5_GATE.md` (S05-02 S6, R39). **Before:** `article_fts` split Devanagari and Bengali words at every vowel sign (सरकार indexed as सरक and र), so a search for सरक matched सरकार and a word could not be found whole. **After:** the index is created with `unicode61 remove_diacritics 2 categories 'L* N* Co M*'`; the word stays one token, accents still fold, CJK and Arabic indexing are unchanged. An existing index keeps its old tokenizer until rebuilt, and the rebuild is paid only where R39 said: inside `migrate-countries`' app-stopped window (estimate first), by an explicit re-index, never at boot. A store already in alpha-3 gets an index-only run. Drop, create and rebuild share one savepoint; a failure after the country flip leaves `fts-pending` with the exception class only.
