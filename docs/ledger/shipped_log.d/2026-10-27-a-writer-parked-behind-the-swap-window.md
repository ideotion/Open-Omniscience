## 2026-10-27 — a writer parked behind the swap window: forced, bounded, ruled out for the shipped app, pinned by an order test (coordinator item 2 of 20:42Z; ruled R338; follows PR #1580; test and ledger only) (PR #1594)

**The question.** The repair's data-version read runs INSIDE the swap window (`_Probe.moved()`: `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` on a connection with a 30 s busy timeout) while every new connection waits behind the window in `connecting()` with no timeout. Can a writer hold SQLite's write lock while parked there?

**Forced.** A writer that holds the lock on a connection made before the window and then asks the guarded engine for a second one: the probe's `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` waited 30.065 s (30.085 s in an earlier run) and failed `database is locked`; the window was held 30.066 s; the writer's second open waited 29.767 s (29.787 s) in `connecting()`. The repair turns any failure of the probe into `Declined("writer-busy")`: nothing is switched, the copy and its sidecar stay, the writer's second connection then opens on the live file. Control (the writer touches its first connection again): refused as stale, rolls back, the probe goes through in 0.33 s (0.3 s of it the writer's own delay). Bounded by the probe's own timeout, no data lost.

**Why the shipped app should not.** `repair.run` holds the single-writer gate for the whole repair, a gated writer takes its connection BEFORE the gate and holds the gate for exactly the life of its write transaction, so no gated writer holds the write lock when the window opens. The writers the gate does not see are the settings saves (one raw `connect()` outside the guard; nothing opens a second connection under it) and the `init_db` DDL steps (launch and unlock, before the boot steps). A sweep of all 1,506 test files with a pool-checkout recorder (136,587 checkouts, 103,477 by a thread already holding another, 668 with the held one in a transaction that had changed rows): all 668 held by ORM sessions, none by Core or raw code; the repair, swap, restore and damage files alone, 669 passed, none. The restore's window waits for no SQLite lock; on Windows its file steps wait up to 300 s for the handle of a thread parked in `connecting()` (the shape the rollup build's boundary check avoids by hand), not exercised.

**Delivered.** `tests/test_repair_probe_parked_writer.py` (the forced case at a 0.5 s busy timeout and the control; an ORDER is asserted, not a duration; mutation-proved against `connecting` replaced by a no-op and against the stale refusal switched off) and the lesson `lessons.d/2026-10-27-a-writer-parked-behind-the-swap-window.md`, which names the options and builds none: a shorter probe timeout (a chosen number), and `connecting()` refusing at once a thread that already holds a connection of the engine (the structural form of the rule the rollup build keeps by hand; R189, a proposal). Raw outputs, the harness and the sweep plugin: the WAL lane's `swap-window-parked-writer-data/`, outside the repository.
