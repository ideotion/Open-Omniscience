## 2026-10-27 — A switch check at the head of a step covers its first request; the recorder reads the switch again before it writes (R325 item 1, R327)

**What was found.** The Export lane's stop-and-record sweep (`/mnt/project-files/export-lane/law-switch-sweep-2026-10-27/REPORT.md`) drove every recorder of a fetch outcome in `src/law/*`, `src/ingest/*` and `src/safety/fetcher.py` with the PRODUCTION fetcher, the switch engaged before the call, cut inside a request, cut and cleared again within one request's flight, and cut on the last retry, with a real 503 as the control. The law tracker and the robots cache were right whenever the switch was still engaged when the failure surfaced (R293, R297, R320 held). Three recorders outside them wrote a Stop down as an outcome: the crawl rung of the housekeeping lane stamped `last_crawled_at` for 3 of 3 outlets with 0 or 1 request, the feed pass stored the ETag and climbed the backoff after a Stop mid-feed, and `advance_backfill` marked a source `done` with 0 URLs when the Stop landed inside the sitemap enumeration. The first two are keyword's files and went to that lane as rows; this entry is the third.

**Why the head check did not help.** `advance_backfill` asks `kill_switch_active()` once at its head and again at the head of each URL, and the lane asks once before it starts. A step that is itself many requests (up to two robots reads and up to 5 sitemaps, 50 with the full history; one URL's fetch, retries and body) is Stop-able in the middle of it, and the callee swallows the refusal on the way out (`declared_sitemap_urls` turns a `FetchError` into `[]`, `discover_sitemap_urls` into an entry in `report.errors`, `ingest_url` into an `IngestOutcome`), so the recorder sees an ordinary failure. An empty enumeration is also a legitimate answer a site can give, and it is recorded as `done` on purpose (`enqueue_source` never re-queues a done source), which is why the two cases must be told apart by the switch and not by the text.

**Rules.**
1. **The recorder reads the switch AFTER the work and BEFORE the write** (the R320 shape: `kill_switch_active()` after the check, never a wider text match). Here: after the enumeration, whatever it returned, a part-read list as well as an empty one, because walking a part-read list as the whole archive records a partial history as complete (`completed_outlets`); and after each URL, only for the two outcomes a Stop can cause (`fetch_failed`, and an exception), so a URL that was stored, a duplicate or a robots block before the Stop keeps its place in the count.
2. **Return before anything is saved.** `advance_backfill` pops the queue entry into a local list and saves the file only on its later paths, so returning without `_save_state` leaves the entry at the head of the queue in the file. A fix that had to put the entry back would have been a second write to get wrong.
3. **Say it the way the other recorders do, not in a new sentence (R327).** The tick that was cut returns `skipped_offline: 1` (R296/R313: a count or `True` key present ONLY when the kill switch caused it; `ingest_source`, `track_watched`, `src/events/feeds.py` carry the same key), on the enumeration cut with `source_id`, and on the final dict of a tick that held URLs back, whether at the head of a URL or after one was cut. The head-of-tick `skipped: "airplane mode engaged"` is the existing sentence and stays; a second refusal sentence beside it is how two surfaces come to disagree about one fact.
4. **The control is a test, not a comment.** An honest empty enumeration with the switch never engaged is still `done` and carries no `skipped_offline`, with the seam and with the real fetcher, and a really failed URL is still passed over and carries none. What the controls add that nothing else kills: the break on `fetch_failed` without the switch, and the real 404 chain ending `done`. (A guard that skips every enumeration is already failed by nine existing tests, so the controls are not what stops that one.)
5. **Prove it with the real fetcher, with seams for the rest.** The enumeration case is also driven through `make_fetcher()` over a scripted session (the Stop lands in the first request and the session raises what the socket guard raises), and the URL case through `ingest_url` for a cut robots read and a cut page. The seam tests cannot show that the chain really ends in an empty list or in `fetch_failed`: if R297's re-raise ever regressed to `RobotsUnavailable` under the switch, every seam test would stay green. What those runs execute is the robots read with its kill-switch branch and the refusals at the head of `declared_sitemaps()` and `fetch()`; a cut in the first request never reaches the retry loop (measured: one `session.get`, no sleep), and the helper replaces `_guard_target`.
6. **Mutants (twelve, eleven red):** no enumeration check; no URL check; the URL check ignoring the outcome (kills "finished before the Stop"); the enumeration check only when the list is empty (kills the part-read case); the URL check on `fetch_failed` only (kills the exception case); the URL check without the switch (kills the existing "a raising URL is tallied" test); the enumeration return without the key; the head-of-URL break without the flag; the URL break without the flag; the flag always set; the old second sentence back. The twelfth, `True` for `1`, is equivalent (`True == 1`). Nine of the twelve new test cases are red against the parent source; the other three are controls.

**Stated limit, not fixed.** A Stop cleared again inside one request's flight is read as the site's failure by every recorder, here too (the robots cache stores "unreachable" for 3600 s; a law document's check is stamped). An engagement epoch bumped by `activate_kill_switch()` and read before and after each request would close it for all readers; it touches every reader and is a proposal on the user list, not part of this change.
