## 2026-10-13 — the log half of a sink guard does not see the record half

The exc_info ratchet (`tests/test_no_raw_exc_info.py`) finds the log calls that ask for a traceback. In `src/bulletin/facts.py`, `src/bulletin/sections.py` and `src/briefing/card_audit.py` the same `except` blocks also wrote the exception's own words into the RECORD they return (`str(exc)`, `f"{type(exc).__name__}: {exc}"`: 14 sites beside 12 log sites), and those records ride the diagnostics bundle. The ratchet cannot see them, so converting only the log call would have left the passphrase's other door open in the very block just fixed. `_leaks` in `tests/test_runs_log_sinks.py` reads the other half: a handler's bound exception may only be handed to a scrubbing call or to `type()`. Rule: when a handler is converted, convert what it RETURNS in the same edit (the record keeps the class; the words go to the scrubbed log line), and list the module in `_SWEEP_MODULES` so both halves are guarded. The same `f"{type(exc).__name__}: {exc}"` shape is still in the other `src/bulletin` modules (annexes, cards, edition, evidence, gate, i18n, introduction, narration, narration_job); they are listed in neither guard until their own conversion.
