## 2026-10-06 — A GATE THAT ESTIMATES RIGHT CAN STILL BE GUARDING THE WRONG DESIGN: THE DIGEST'S 1,062 MiB WAS CORRECT AND AVOIDABLE (keyword-export thread, 2026-10-06, `keywords.py`, `keyword_log_scan.py`)

Four of the 17 machines in the 2026-10-05/06 diagnostics (OOS-10, OOS-11, OOS-12, hP) declined `keyword-log-digest.json` on its memory, and the first suspicion was the gate's estimate. It was
right: 1,062 MiB estimated against 1,057 MiB measured on a synthetic 82-language database (the shape of the largest instance's export). What was wrong was that the digest needed that much.
Measured by phase (the route's own `build_families` and the digest loop wrapped, RSS read at each): 59 MiB after the scan, 285 MiB after the entries, a transient +535 MiB inside
`build_families` over 410,000 items, and 200 MiB more for the dict of every family (`Family.to_dict` carries each variant) kept so a summary could be computed over all of them. The first
version of the fix (metadata fetched a batch at a time) cut only 1,057 to 858: the metadata and signatures were about a fifth of the peak (199 of 1,057 MiB), the entries built from them (226), the grouping and the dicts were the rest. What stayed after three
changes, each held by a test: entries built a batch at a time (`_DigestPass`); the families grouped over the archive's memory-sized basis (a tenth of the available memory at 2.5 KB a keyword,
floor 50,000, disclosed in `families_provenance` and in the summary's own flags; every other block still covers the whole window); the summary taken from seven light fields of each `Family` instead of every family as a
dict. Rise at 410,000 entries: 1,057 MiB before; 330 with 2 GB free, 253 with 1.2 GB, 490 with 4 GB, 816 with 16 GB after (27 timed runs over 21, 41 and 82 languages and four availability
readings; the model `fixed 60 MiB + entries x 500 B + grouped x 2,500 B + the article arrays` covers every one, by 1.3 to 1.6 times over the nine points of the 21-, 41- and 82-language grids and by 1.34 to 1.44 over the 82-language ones, medians of the runs at each point; those runs budgeted the grouping at 2,000 B a keyword, so they grouped a quarter more than the code now does). The gate now admits OOS-12, hP and the 091717 reading and
declines OOS-11, whose 425 MiB free is why it should. **Reusable:** (1) when an estimate matches the measurement, the next question is which part of the peak exists only because something is
held, and the answer comes from RSS read at the phase boundaries, not from the estimate; (2) a need that depends on a budget derived from the machine's free memory (here the grouping's)
must take the SAME reading the gate takes, or the two disagree about one quantity; (3) a differential harness against the code being replaced (`differential_keyword_export_normalised.py`, a copy of the shared
`differential_keyword_export.py` that also sets aside the six keys the change added to the digest: four in `families_provenance`, two in `families_summary`) is what makes a rewrite of a streamed document safe; (4) do not touch a worktree (a `git stash` for a baseline) while a
measurement grid runs from it: one run in 27 read 1,004 MiB beside its neighbours' 318 because it started on the old code, and only the comparison with its repeats showed it; (5) the independent read of this change found what the author's own checks had passed: a provenance note that said "Nothing is DROPPED" and a summary whose method said "computed over ALL families" stayed printed when the new budget cut the grouping to a prefix (716 of 738 conflated families missing, the sentences unchanged), so a claim written for one regime must be CONDITIONAL on the regime that decides it, and the flag belongs on the block a reader may see alone; and the margin "because real terms are longer" had been put on the one constant that holds no term (a survivor row is ids, counts and dates), where it protected nothing, while the constant that scales with a term had none: put a margin on the quantity it is for, then pin that constant by value against a resident measurement of the worst shape (Cyrillic phrases, 2,228 B a grouped keyword), not only against a tracemalloc lower bound.
