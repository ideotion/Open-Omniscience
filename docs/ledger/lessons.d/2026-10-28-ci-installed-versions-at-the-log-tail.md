## 2026-10-28 — the installed versions are printed at the END of a CI job, by a step that runs on red: an install prints them at the head of a log that a tail-limited reader never reaches

**What was found (R349, R364).** Finding the cause of the `test_page_reading` red took a comparison of installed packages between a green and a red job, and the packages are listed by the install step, near the first 100 lines of a log that runs to several thousand. The one log reader available in the agent sessions (`get_job_logs`) returns the last 5000 lines, and the blob host that serves a whole log answers 403 through the proxy, so the install output of the long `test` job could not be read at all. The comparison was rebuilt from three sibling jobs that were short enough to have a complete log (86 to 104 packages each) plus PyPI's upload time for the one package that differed (pytest 9.2.0, 21:30 UTC on 27 Oct) and a local reproduction under both versions. That worked, but only because those siblings existed; for the next moving part they may not.

**The rule.** A CI job whose red can come from the environment ends with a step that prints the environment, and that step says `if: always()`: without the condition a red pytest step skips it, and the red run is the one that needs it. It goes straight after the pytest step, before any step that installs a scanner of its own (bandit and pip-audit in `test`), so the list is the one the tests ran in. It uses `python -m pip list --format=freeze` (one `name==version` line per package; `pip freeze` prints the editable install as a path on the runner) and also writes to `$GITHUB_STEP_SUMMARY`, where the list survives the log. The header line carries the Python patch version, the runner image and the kernel, the other things R349 had to diff.

**What it costs, and what it does not prove.** About 112 lines and 2.0 KB in the `test` job and 91 lines and 1.6 KB in Core-only; with the runner's 29-byte timestamp on each line and the echo of the step's own script, about 6 KB of the log (INFERRED: a `uv pip compile` of the same extras on 2026-10-28, 108 and 87 packages; the measured figure from the first CI run is in the PR body). It lands inside the 5000-line tail on red (nothing after it runs) and, INFERRED and not counted, on green (the later mypy, bandit, pip-audit and post steps print well under a thousand lines in the sibling logs). It lists the packages; it does not say which of them moved. The comparison between two runs is still made by a person or a script, and the Core-only and `test` jobs are the only two it covers: the other lanes install less and run a named subset.

**Pinned by** `tests/test_ci_installed_versions_step.py`: the step exists once in each of the two jobs, says `if: always()`, runs the freeze command, writes the summary and sits straight after the last pytest step; seven mutants per job (condition removed or `failure()`, `pip freeze`, no summary, step missing, step before pytest, a step between) are each named by the checker.
