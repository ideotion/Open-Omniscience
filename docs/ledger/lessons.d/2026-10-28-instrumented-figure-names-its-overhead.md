## 2026-10-28 — an instrumented figure names its overhead or carries the unperturbed figure beside it: a per-statement SQL timer on 9.9 M cursor calls costs 35 to 50 percent of the wall it reports

**What was found (R342, RC2's note, R347).** The cold `GET /api/diagnostics/keywords` and `GET /api/diagnostics/performance` figures taken by WAL with a per-statement SQL timer on (46.2 s and 80.9 s) were re-taken by RC2 on a rebuilt store of the same shape (300,000 articles, 30,000 keywords, 9,900,000 mentions; encrypted, 3,377,422,336 B; SQLCipher 4.12.0, SQLite 3.51.1; a quiet 4-vCPU box; cold = `fadvise DONTNEED` plus a fresh process) with the timer off. Unperturbed and cold: `/keywords` 30.8 s (one run), `/performance` 59.3, 59.8 and 58.7 s. The instrumented figures match WAL's (46.2 s against 38.9 s; 80.9 s against 79.7 s) because the timer is called once per cursor step, and 9.9 M of those cost 35 to 50 percent of the wall.

**The rule.** A figure taken with instrumentation on names that instrumentation and what it costs, or carries the unperturbed figure beside it. A reader who sees only the instrumented number cannot tell which part of it is the code and which part is the ruler.

**What the unperturbed figure does and does not change.** The split of the wall between blocks (about 81 percent of `/keywords` and 90 percent of `/performance` is keyword's own mechanism: `scan_keywords`, `fetch_signatures`, `filters.hidden_set`) holds on both. On `/performance` the `keyword_export_streamed` probe runs as two blocks, 28.2 s and 24.5 s unperturbed (the R321 shape), and the five other probes sum to about 4.5 s; on `/keywords`, `scan_keywords` is 23.9 s, `ArticleMaps` 1.1 s and the remainder about 5.7 s. Not measured: spread on `/keywords` (one run), a real Pareto corpus, plain against encrypted. One route-level cost is not keyword's: `performance.py:198` runs the export probe twice per request (once against cold, once against warm), which is a proposal for the user (OPEN_QUEUE item (19)), not a change.
