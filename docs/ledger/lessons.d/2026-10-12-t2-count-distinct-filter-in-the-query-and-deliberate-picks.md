## 2026-10-12 — A READ-TIME HIDE MUST ACT BEFORE THE CUT AND INSIDE A DISTINCT COUNT, AND MUST NOT APPLY THE LISTING'S LENGTH RULE TO A DELIBERATE PICK (R111 step T2 rest, #1340)

(1) **A hide that runs after a cut or a limit returns fewer rows than asked for, or none.** The bulletin's coverage column took `limit * 4` rows by mention count and filtered afterwards: eight stoplisted LaTeX macros outranking every real word left an empty column. The fetch now widens (x4) until it holds the headroom in VISIBLE rows or the window has no more rows; a test with only hidden rows pins that the loop stops. The same shape in `stories.article_keyword_sets` let a hidden word take one of an article's twelve slots and, repeated in two unrelated articles, join them into a story (Jaccard 4/8 with the hidden words counted, 0/4 without).
(2) **Where a query counts DISTINCT articles, the filter belongs in the query.** `derive_source_topics` is `COUNT(DISTINCT article)` per (domain, topic); an article carrying two keywords of one topic is counted once. Subtracting a hidden keyword's count afterwards counts that article twice or not at all. The tagged keywords are few, so their hidden ids are resolved first and left out with `NOT IN` in chunks of 900 (SQLite's variable limit).
(3) **One measure, two resolvers: change both or the panel and the sky show two numbers.** `resolve_member_keyword_ids` (Observatory, rising test, stats, drill-through) and `_supergroup_totals` (the Groups panel) turn members into keyword ids separately; a test resolves one group through both against one corpus and asserts equal id sets.
(4) **The listing's `is_hidden` (stoplist + user exclusions + shorter than `min_length` + numeric) is the wrong predicate for a deliberate pick.** A group member and a topic tag are chosen on purpose; `ai` is both and has two letters, so the length rule silently emptied the AI family and eight existing tests said so. Group and topic readers hide by the LIST only (`filters.hidden_set()`); the bulletin, which is a listing, uses the full predicate. Measured: 13 terms of the shipped rings were already on the shipped list (`news` in news-media, `work` in occupation ...), so the earlier «curated-group totals matter only if a curated member is later stoplisted» was already false.
