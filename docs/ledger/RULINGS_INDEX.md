# Rulings index — one line per ruling (created 2026-09-15 per Q1206)

**What this is.** The flat index of every maintainer ruling recorded from the 2026-09-12 roadmap round onward,
one line each: `id · date · ruling · source · where enforced`. It exists so that nothing is restated: before asking
the maintainer anything, grep here (and `OPEN_QUEUE.md`, and `shipped.csv`) — the ledger is the only authority on
whether a ruling exists. Maintained under THE PROTOCOL (`CLAUDE.md`, amended 2026-09-15): every new ruling gets a
line here in the turn it is given, beside its `OPEN_QUEUE.md` entry.

**Sources.** `R1`–`R19` are the maintainer's two messages of 2026-09-12 (later chat rulings continue the series, dated in their row — `R20` is 2026-09-18); `A1`–`L10` (the second table) are the
65 answers of the 2026-09-06 register (`docs/plans/2026-09-06-repo-analysis/QUESTIONS_FOR_THE_MAINTAINER.md`)
given through the rulings artifact on 2026-09-15 15:02–16:00Z — each reconciled with the `Q11xx` row that re-asked
it (a CONFLICT is listed on both rows, never resolved here; `RCnn` points at the confirmation round
`docs/design/RULINGS_CONFIRMATION_2026-09-15_REGISTER_ROUND.md`); `Qnnn` rows are the answers to
[`docs/design/ROADMAP_ANSWER_SHEET_2026-09-12_BETA_PATHWAY.md`](../design/ROADMAP_ANSWER_SHEET_2026-09-12_BETA_PATHWAY.md)
(the primary record; the label after the letter is that option's label verbatim, the note is the maintainer's
note verbatim). `RC01`–`RC17` are the CONFIRMATION ROUND
(`docs/design/RULINGS_CONFIRMATION_2026-09-15_REGISTER_ROUND.md`), which came back with **0 of its 22 `ANSWER` lines carrying a letter** — so each RC row records what the round's own §0 makes of a BLANK: PENDING on a ⛔ (and on `RC12`, which states no default), a labelled ASSUMPTION at the stated default otherwise. An RC ASSUMPTION never resolves a CONFLICT: both answers stay on their `A1`–`L10` and `Qnnn` rows and the assumption is reversed by writing the letter.
`source` says how each Q row was obtained: **answered** · **default** (blank, the sheet's stated
default, an ASSUMPTION the maintainer may reverse at any time) · **pending** (blank on a ⛔ question — never
defaulted, not a ruling). `where enforced` names the release and slice brief that carries the ruling (the gate
files under `docs/product/` and the briefs under `docs/plans/2026-09-12-beta-pathway/`), or the test that already
pins it. Rulings before 2026-09-12 are NOT back-filled here (they live in `OPEN_QUEUE.md`); back-filling them is
recorded as a 0.4 docs item, not done.

**Conventions.** ⛔ = never taken autonomously; 🔒 = changes what the app retains or contacts; CONFLICT = two answers
contradict and both are recorded as given (see the 2026-09-15 `OPEN_QUEUE.md` entry for the list); TENSION = the
maintainer's note overrides the option label (the note is the ruling).
