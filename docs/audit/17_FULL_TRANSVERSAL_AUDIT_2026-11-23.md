## 1. Performance — the commissioned emphasis

Backup import and post-import re-indexing are measurably the slow parts of the app, at every scale tested.

- **PERF-1 · P0 — the restore's swap barrier waits minutes on a lease the app itself holds.** [MEASURED] Restoring a **2,000-article** backup took **158.4 s wall, of which 153.6 s was `time.sleep`** inside `wait_for_quiescence` (`src/backup/merge.py:10009`, `_SWAP_QUIESCE_S = 180.0` at `merge.py:85`; `src/database/corpus_lease.py:232`). The actual data merge in that run took **0.74 s** (`merge_corpus`, `merge.py:2242`). At **20,000 articles** the same restore **exceeded 570 s** (timed out), while the identical restore with only the wait neutralised took **17.5 s** (1,143 art/s). The wait polled the leases directory **619 times** in one 150 s window (`active_leases` → `_scan`, `corpus_lease.py:128-221`), each poll a glob + read + stat pass. In a second run the restore of a **500-article** backup **hard-refused after 180 s** with "another job is still writing to your corpus (**reindex-resume**) … nothing was written" — the lease named is taken by the app's own re-index drain (`src/api/backup_v2.py:1536`, auto-started at boot by `src/api/boot_sequence.py:419`).
- **PERF-2 · P0 — the post-import re-index drain runs at ~6–10 articles/second.** [MEASURED] `reindex_imported_articles` (`src/backup/merge.py:8754`) → `src/analytics/store.py::reindex_articles`: 500 articles → **51–90 s**; 2,000 articles → **190.9 s** (10.5 art/s). cProfile of a 500-article run: **~20.0 M function calls (~40,000 calls per article)**; top of the profile is waiting, not computing: `lock.acquire` **57.0 s**, `select.poll` **15.7 s**, `posix.write` cum **43.0 s**, `index_article` (`store.py:767`) cum 10.9 s in the parent only. [ARITHMETIC] At the field-reported 1.28 M imported articles (audit 15), 10 art/s ⇒ **~35 h**, and the measured field rate on Qubes VMs was 50–61 h without finishing (audit 16) — same order of magnitude; the drain is the dominant post-import cost.
- **PERF-3 · P1 — the per-article write path degrades as the corpus grows.** [MEASURED] Seeding through the ORM (FTS triggers live): 2,000 articles → **1,800 art/s**; 20,000 articles → **1,190 art/s** on the same machine (8 MB vs 70 MB DB). Consistent with the "random-write storm across ~20 B-trees plus FTS" reading in audit 15 §F1.
- **PERF-4 · P1 — read endpoints are already slow at a small corpus.** [MEASURED] Live server on the 20,000-article corpus (loopback, plaintext): `GET /api/insights/corpus-coordination` **1,920 ms**, `GET /api/insights/trending` **399 ms**, `GET /api/search/omni?q=go` (2-char) first hit **253–529 ms** (37–42 ms warm), `GET /api/health` first hit **928 ms**, `GET /api/jobs` **117 ms**; fast ones: `/api/search/instant` 24 ms, `/api/timemap` 41 ms, `/api/insights/status` 55 ms. [CODE-READ] the omni route's own docstring (`src/api/search_omni.py:642`) states it runs **2× full FTS `search_ids` per debounced keystroke** and that on a large corpus a 2-char term matches most of it — i.e. PERF-4 scales with corpus size. Production instances run 500 k–1.3 M articles (audits 15/16), 25–65× this fixture.
- **PERF-5 · P2 — for contrast, the parts that are fine:** [MEASURED] backup export 70 MB in **2.5 s** (28 MB/s); `merge_corpus` alone 12–14 s for 2 k–20 k (11.7 s of 12.4 s inside raw `sqlite3` `execute` — SQL-bound, not Python-bound); FTS `MATCH` query **0.11–0.47 ms** at 20 k articles.

## 4. Functional

- **FUNC-1 · P1 — an import can refuse after 180 s and send the operator back to square one.** [MEASURED] The 500-article restore above ended HTTP 400: "another job is still writing to your corpus (**reindex-resume**) after waiting 180 s — nothing was written to your corpus. Stop that job, or let it finish, and **import again**." The named lease is the app's own auto-resumed drain (`src/api/boot_sequence.py:419` → `src/backup/volume_job.py::start_reindex_drain`), so on a fresh boot the import and the boot drain contend for the same lease — a repeat-import loop is possible. [CODE-READ]
- **FUNC-2 · P2 — the re-index of an imported batch can end in an opaque driver error.** [MEASURED] First run of `reindex_imported_articles` over the imported 2,000-article batch completed its work (2,032 keywords / 160,000 mentions created) and then raised `IntegrityError: its text is withheld (a database driver's words)` on an `executemany`; a direct `reindex_articles` run also reported `failed: 1` for one of 500 articles with no surfaced reason. The error text is deliberately withheld by the app's scrubbing, which is right for secrets but leaves the operator with no diagnosable cause. [HYPOTHESIS] fixture-specific (synthetic random-word bodies) — needs one confirmation on a real corpus.
- **FUNC-3 · info — gates green:** blocking ruff, mypy, bandit, targeted pytest all as above; `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`'s "PostgreSQL is unsupported scaffolding" claim verified against `src/database/session.py` (FTS no-ops on non-SQLite) and the pyproject floor comments. [CODE-READ]

- **OPT-1 · P2 —** the measured hot paths cluster in exactly three places: the swap quiesce wait (`src/backup/merge.py:10007-10016` + `src/database/corpus_lease.py`), the per-article re-index (`src/analytics/store.py:767,2029` + `src/analytics/reindex_parallel.py`), and per-article ingest writes (`src/ingest/batch.py` + FTS triggers in `src/database/fts.py`).
