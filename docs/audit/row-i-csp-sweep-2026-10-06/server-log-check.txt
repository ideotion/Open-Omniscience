# Server-log check of the sweeps. Two boots of the same offline app, run on the logs as they stood at the time of the check.
# The logs themselves are not committed (access-log lines, tens of thousands of them); the commands and their real outputs are below.
# (The uniq -c leading padding was trimmed in the pasted output; the grep -v is a substring filter on the extracted URL, so "host lacks 127.0.0.1 or localhost" is what it tests.)
# What the greps can see: lines of the log. They cannot see a packet; they say no http(s) URL naming a host other than loopback appears,
# that the boot line says airplane mode, and that no error, exception or traceback was logged.
